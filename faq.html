Common Queries

How do I register?

Open the Register page and fill in the required personal and academic details in the form.

How do I login?

Navigate to the Login page and enter your registered Student ID and Password.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page to fill out the support form or find administrative contact details.

Can I update my profile?

Yes, you can manage and review your academic information directly from the Profile page.
